Flat Design: Clean, minimalist, and focuses on two-dimensional elements without extra shadows or gradients.

Skeuomorphism: Interfaces mimic real-world objects/textures to create familiarity (modern usage is often more subdued).

Retro Futurism: Nostalgic, inspired by past visions of the future—bright colors, pixel art, or arcade-like visuals.

Cyberpunk: Futuristic, dark backgrounds, neon colors, hologram-like elements, and sharp grids.

Bauhaus: Inspired by the Bauhaus movement—geometric shapes, bold typography, primary colors.

Brutalism: Raw, bold, and sometimes intentionally "imperfect" layouts with strong grids and large typography.

Minimalism: Extreme simplicity, emphasizing whitespace and limiting visible interface elements.

Glassmorphism: Glass-like, frosted transparency for interface cards, overlays, and UI elements.

Skeuomorphism/Modern Skeuomorphism: Realistic objects and textures, sometimes blended with modern minimalism.

Bento Grids: Modular layouts inspired by Japanese “bento boxes,” allowing flexible arrangement of interface blocks.

3D/Clay Morphism: Soft, extruded shapes and icons with tactile, playful 3D effects (sometimes called “claymorphism”).

Dark Mode: Interface optimized for low-light environments, often with accent colors for readability.

Bold Typography: Focused on strong, oversized fonts for messages, navigation, and calls to action.
